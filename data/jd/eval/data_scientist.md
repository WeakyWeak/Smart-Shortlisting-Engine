# Data Science Intern
**Quantly Analytics**

## Responsibilities
- Build predictive models and evaluate them against held-out data.
- Clean, transform and analyse large datasets.
- Produce dashboards and visualisations for business stakeholders.
- Run hypothesis tests and communicate statistical findings.

## Required Skills
- Strong Python programming for data analysis.
- Experience with pandas and numpy.
- Machine learning with scikit-learn.
- SQL for querying relational data.
- Data visualization with matplotlib or seaborn.
- Understanding of regression and classification techniques.
- Statistics and hypothesis testing.

## Preferred Skills
- Experience with TensorFlow or PyTorch.
- Dashboarding in Power BI or Tableau.
- Jupyter Notebook workflow.
- Exposure to NLP or computer vision.
